## What Builder is
Builder is an AI participant in a video call. It listens to a group of mostly business users describing an app, steers
the discussion the way a facilitator would, and builds a clickable prototype live on a screenshare. When people
disagree, it shows the options side by side. When the call ends, engineers get a link with the prototype, the component
spec and the decision log. The full v0 design is in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-meeting-prototype-agent-design.md`.

## Next use case (not started)
